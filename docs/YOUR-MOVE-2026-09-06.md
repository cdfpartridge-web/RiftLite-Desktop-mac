## Earlier proposed creator flow — implemented locally above

1. Open a replay in the existing web player and choose Create Your Move at the desired decision.
2. Pin the exact replay event, choose the player perspective, and select where the revealed continuation should end.
3. Add one question and an interaction: select cards for mulligan, or choose from two to four authored answers for other decisions. Do not promise simulation of alternative game states.
4. Review automatically supplied matchup, game, turn, initiative, score and visible card context. Add optional short context, a hint and a private-until-reveal explanation. Unknown data must remain unknown.
5. Preview as a fresh viewer; save a draft or explicitly publish a public/unlisted challenge.

Own replays are the starting point. Proposed support for other people's recordings uses an owner-controlled Allow challenges setting with separate credits for the challenge author and recording owner. Do not silently treat a replay's public visibility as permission to redistribute its hidden data. A challenge references the original replay; publication, access, deletion and revocation rules must respect source permissions.

## Proposed authoring content and presentation

- **Before answering:** short question, concise situational note, automatically derived visible match context, optional hint, optional card highlights/arrows that contain no future information.
- **After answering:** the viewer's choice beside the recorded choice, actual continuation, creator explanation, optional notes on each alternative and later optional voice explanation with text equivalent.
- Label creator explanations as that author's analysis. A third-party challenge author must not appear to speak for the original player. The recorded play is not automatically the optimal play or the answer to every puzzle.
- Keep the real board central. On desktop, use an adjacent question panel; on smaller screens place the question below the board or hand. Put longer context in an expandable panel. After locking, transform the same question panel into the reveal and playback action.
- A compact top row identifies the author, source player, matchup and challenge topic. Automatically derive factual metadata and let authors add difficulty/topic tags without making these authoritative skill assessments.
- V1 should be one question per challenge with card selection or a few authored options, text explanation, preview, source credit and direct sharing. Multiple decision chains, recorded voice, community voting, collections and creator analytics are later candidates.

## Proposed sharing and embeds

- A dedicated challenge link, with a preview image/title that does not reveal the answer.
- Public and unlisted sharing choices; a private draft remains private. A private source cannot become public accidentally via its challenge.
- Copy embed code for a responsive interactive challenge on sites that support third-party embeds. Keep RiftLite branding, source credit and Open in RiftLite available. Platforms without interactive embed support receive a linked preview instead.
- Existing `src/pages/replays/[replayId].tsx` accepts an embed flag, but the current `next.config.mjs` applies `X-Frame-Options: DENY` globally. It does not currently provide general third-party website embedding. Implement a dedicated challenge embed route and scoped framing policy; do not weaken framing restrictions across account/application pages.
- Challenge records should reference the replay ID, exact event identity and replay revision, perspective, continuation range, prompt, options, notes and author/source attribution. Validate stale or missing source events instead of silently moving a challenge to a different decision.
