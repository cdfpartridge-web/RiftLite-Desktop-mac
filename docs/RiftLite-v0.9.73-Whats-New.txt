RIFTLITE BETA v0.9.73
ATLAS DECK EDITING AND SIGNED ARTWORK

- Edit Deck and New Deck on Atlas now stay inside RiftLite.
- Use Back to Play to return to the lobby. Save in Atlas first; the return button does not save your deck.
- When Atlas reports unsaved changes, choose Stay or Leave without saving.
- Fixes the collapsed Atlas Player-name field that could persist in v0.9.72.
- Adds all nine missing signed Vendetta variants and improves alternate-art handling in desktop card and replay views.
- Updates the default Shadowblade Lurker artwork.
- Improves replay-cache, cloud-backup, playback, export and embedded Atlas lifecycle handling.
- Removes accumulated obsolete build files from new installers.

Your existing profile, matches, decks, replays, settings and media paths are preserved.
Replay Coach remains Coming Soon. Deck Insights remains available. Search Rules remains hidden.
The local Your Move website demo and future training ideas are separate from this desktop update.

macOS installers are ad-hoc signed and not Apple-notarized; the standard first-open warning may appear.
Signed-in Atlas deck saving and live gameplay acceptance remain pending beyond automated release checks.
